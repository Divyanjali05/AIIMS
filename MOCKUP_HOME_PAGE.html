Design & Hierarchy Rationale (v3 Specification Alignment):
  • Information Hierarchy: The home page places the Mentor & Mascot card at the top to orient the learner with contextual advice, followed by the two primary active cards (Baseline Diagnostic and Proposed Focus Mode), leading down to audience-filtered tool recommendations.
  • Audience Toggle: The Student / Entrepreneur switch updates tool badges and recommendation criteria dynamically without requiring page navigation.
  • Accessibility (WCAG 2.1 AA): High-contrast color tokens (#0f172a on #ffffff = 16:1 ratio), 48px touch targets on mobile, clear semantic focus rings, and explicit text descriptions.
  • Mobile Behavior: Collapses the sidebar into a native bottom navigation bar, stacks two-column cards into a vertical flow, and retains critical wallet balance chip in sticky header.
  • No Unsupported Claims: Zero fabricated user counts ("10,000+ students"), zero fake testimonials, and zero unverified grant claims. Proposed features (Focus Mode, 500 AC balance) are clearly labeled.
